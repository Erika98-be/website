Open Frame Studio

From frame drawing to cutting list and IFC, in one free app

Pick a template, divide the frame into bays and give each bay a door, sash, glass or panel. You see it in 3D straight away, together with the production lists and the Uw value.

Features

Everything for the frame, from drawing to workshop

Start with the highlights, or pick a topic.

01 Free layout

Divide the frame the way you want

Split a bay with a mullion or transom and choose a door, sash, fixed glass, panel or spandrel for each bay, with glazing bead and opening direction. Start from a door with side light, a door with transom light or a grid.

Learn more

02 3D view

See the frame in the wall

The 3D view follows your drawing: real profiles, glass in the rebate, sashes and doors as separate parts and the brickwork around them. You can check at a glance whether it is right.

Learn more

03 Production

The cutting list follows the drawing

Every jamb, sill and sash member is in the cutting list, with net and gross length and cutting angle. There are also lists for glass, hardware, gaskets, panels and glazing beads. Joints follow joinery practice: mortise and tenon for wood, mitred for aluminium and PVC.

Learn more

04 Energy

Know straight away whether the Uw value fits

For each frame the app calculates the Uw value, the area and the transmission loss from the profiles and the glazing. Set a limit and see at once which frame meets it.

Learn more

05 Profiles

Work with your own profiles

Pick from the library of wood, aluminium, PVC and wood-aluminium, or draw a cross-section point by point. Set width, depth, rebate and Uf value, or import a profile from DXF.

Learn more

  • 2D frame editor

    Columns and rows, drag handles and a closed chain of dimensions, with undo and redo.

  • Templates

    Tilt-and-turn, double, French window, top-hung, pivot, front door, sliding and lift-and-slide door. Save your own frame as a template.

  • Free layout

    Split, merge or swap bays and choose the infill, glazing bead, opening direction and hardware per bay.

  • Special shapes

    Segmental arch, round, trapezoid or an arch on sloping jambs, besides the usual rectangular frame.

  • 3D view

    Extruded profiles, glass in the rebate and the brickwork around it, also for stepped shapes such as a door with side light.

  • RAL colours

    A separate colour for the inside and outside, shown in 2D and 3D.

  • Dutch drawing conventions

    Dimensioning to NEN 3576 and opening symbols in the outside view to VKG and EN 12519.

  • Profile library

    Wood in standard KVT sizes (meranti, accoya, pine, oak, FSC hardwood), aluminium, PVC and wood-aluminium, with sash, door and glazing-bead profiles.

  • Well-known systems

    Templates for VEKA, Kömmerling, Reynaers CS 77 and Schüco AWS 75.SI+, among others, with mitred corners.

  • Profile editor

    Draw a cross-section point by point, with mirroring, snap grid and undo.

  • Profile from DXF

    Import a supplier's cross-section from a DXF file.

  • Corner joints

    Mortise and tenon, mitre, scribed or butt joint, set per corner. Your own choice always wins.

  • Production lists

    Cutting list with net and gross lengths and cutting angles, plus lists for glass, hardware, gaskets, panels, glazing beads and parts.

  • Frame schedule

    All frames with mark, dimensions, material, bays, glazing and Ug value. The desktop app turns it into a PDF or Excel file.

  • Workshop drawing

    A3 drawings with title block, and DXF with dimension lines for the workshop, from the desktop app.

  • Uw per frame

    Uw value, area and transmission loss per frame, checked against a limit you set yourself.

  • Glazing per bay

    Choose the glass per bay, such as double or triple glazing, with the Ug value that counts towards the Uw.

  • IFC export

    IFC 4 with the ILS Houten Kozijnen v2.0 property sets, for your BIM model. The desktop app also reads frames from IFC.

  • glTF and CSV

    The 3D model as glTF for visualisation, and the production data as CSV for your own software.

  • Blender and Bonsai

    Send a frame from the desktop app straight to Blender with Bonsai to continue modelling in IFC.

  • AI assistant

    Type "Make a tilt-and-turn 900×1400" and the frame appears. Works with OpenAI, Ollama or another compatible endpoint.

  • Three languages

    The app works in Dutch, English and German, with dimensions in millimetres.

  • Light and dark

    A light and a dark theme, in the style of the other OpenAEC apps.

Frequently asked questions

Which files can I open?

Your own projects in .ofs. The desktop app also reads frames from an IFC file and profiles from DXF.

Do I need to install anything?

Not to try it out: the web version works right in the browser. To save and to export to IFC, DXF, PDF and Excel you need the desktop app; the browser cannot do that yet.

What does Open Frame Studio cost?

Nothing. The app is free and open source under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence, with no account or subscription.

In which format is my work saved?

In its own .ofs project file. The desktop app also exports to IFC, DXF, PDF, Excel, CSV and glTF.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows (also without admin rights), macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon) and Linux (.deb, .rpm and AppImage), and in a modern browser.

Does it fit Dutch practice?

Yes. Dimensioning to NEN 3576, opening symbols to VKG and EN 12519, wood profiles in KVT sizes and IFC with the ILS Houten Kozijnen v2.0 property sets.

What can an AI assistant do with it?

The app has a built-in AI assistant that creates and edits frames from what you type. You connect it to OpenAI, Ollama or another compatible endpoint. There is no MCP server for external assistants yet.

Can I draw a curtain wall too?

In the desktop app, yes. The browser version cannot do that yet.

Can I already order frames with it?

It is an early version (0.6). Check dimensions and lists before you order or start cutting, and report errors on GitHub.

Technology

For those who want to know how it is built.

Rust core

The data model, geometry and all exports (IFC, DXF, PDF, glTF, XLSX, CSV) live in one Rust library, with over a hundred tests.

Tauri 2

A thin native shell for Windows, macOS and Linux, with small installers.

Svelte 5 · Vite

The same interface on the desktop and in the browser, with a 2D editor in SVG.

Three.js

The 3D view with extruded profiles and glass, right in the app.

WebAssembly

The Rust core runs as WebAssembly in the browser version.

IFC 4 · ILS

Open exchange with BIM software, following the Dutch ILS Houten Kozijnen v2.0.
