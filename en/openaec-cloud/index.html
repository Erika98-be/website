Files and collaboration

OpenAEC SuperCloud

All your project files in sync on every computer, and safely shared with whoever you choose

Link a folder on your computer to a project space in your OpenAEC environment and keep working in your own programs. The OpenAEC Desktop App keeps the files in sync, shares them with a link and sends large files without a separate service.

Features

Your project folders, in sync everywhere and properly shared

Start with the highlights, or pick a topic.

01 Sync

Work in an ordinary folder, the rest happens on its own

As soon as you save a file, it goes to the project space, and your colleagues' changes come in on their own. For a large change, only the changed parts are transferred. Choose per space which folders come to this computer: the site photos can simply stay on the server.

Learn more

02 Spaces

One space per project, with the people who belong to it

Each space has members with a role: manager, writer, reader or guest. Browse the files on the server, see who has locked a file and create new folders. Folders that another party shares with you are listed under Shared with me, and you link them just as easily.

Learn more

03 Sharing

Share with a link or with a role

Choose Share on a file or folder, in the app or in File Explorer, and create a link for someone without an account, with a password and an expiry date. Give a colleague the role Can view, Can edit or Can manage. Under Who has access you change a role or withdraw access again.

Learn more

04 Sending

Send large files without a transfer service

Choose Send with OpenAEC in the right-click menu, also outside your project folders. The recipient gets a download link, by e-mail if you like, with a message, an expiry date and a password. If the connection drops, you resume where you were; under My transfers you see the downloads and withdraw a link.

Learn more

05 Assistant

Ask the assistant, with your files attached

Drag a PDF, Word or Excel file, text or image into the conversation and ask your question. The questions go to OpenAEC's AI service in the EU and use your environment's AI credit. Your conversations stay on your own computer.

Learn more

06 Tools

Keep all OpenAEC apps up to date in one place

The Tools tab shows which OpenAEC apps you have and which ones have a new version. Install, update and start with one click, even without a connection to your environment. The app only fetches releases from OpenAEC-Foundation and checks every download.

  • Two-way sync

    A local folder and a space stay the same: as soon as you save something and as soon as the server reports something new.

  • Only what has changed

    For a large model, only the changed blocks are transferred; the app shows how much that saves.

  • No work lost in a conflict

    If two people worked on the same file at the same time, both versions are kept.

  • Choose folders

    Per space you decide which folders come to this computer, and whether new folders come along automatically.

  • One folder in File Explorer

    On Windows, OpenAEC Cloud sits in the navigation pane, with a subfolder per space.

  • Files on demand

    On Windows, new files are online-only at first and come in as soon as you open them. You can free up space too.

  • Status on every file

    On Windows, File Explorer shows per file and folder whether it is up to date, busy or online-only.

  • Progress and storage

    What is being transferred, the speed, the time remaining and how much storage your environment has left. Pause from the system tray.

  • Ignore list and awkward names

    Temporary files stay out, your own patterns go in .openaecignore, and names Windows cannot handle are skipped cleanly.

  • Spaces with roles

    A space per project or department, with members as manager, writer, reader or guest.

  • New space with folder layout

    An environment manager creates a space from the app, with a fixed folder layout as a template.

  • Shared with me

    Link folders from other parties like a space. With read-only access nothing goes to the server.

  • Browse files on the server

    Browse a space without linking it, create folders and see who has locked a file.

  • Locking and versions

    Lock a file so colleagues see you are working on it, and open an earlier version.

  • Link for outsiders

    View and download without an account, with password and expiry date. If it is already in OpenAEC, nothing is uploaded.

  • Share with a role

    Can view, Can edit or Can manage. You never grant more than you have yourself.

  • Send with OpenAEC

    Files and whole folders with a download link, from the right-click menu in File Explorer, Finder, Nautilus, Dolphin or Nemo.

  • Resume and withdraw

    An interrupted transfer continues where it left off. Under My transfers you see the downloads and withdraw a link.

  • Assistant with attachments

    Questions about PDFs, Word and Excel files, text and images, through OpenAEC's AI service in the EU.

  • Conversations on your own computer

    Every conversation is stored locally and can be deleted. After each answer you see the usage.

  • Install OpenAEC apps

    Install, update, start and remove, with a check of every download on Windows and Linux.

  • Updates itself

    The app downloads new versions in the background and only installs them when you click, never in the middle of a sync.

  • Command line for servers

    The openaec-storage program syncs spaces on Linux without a screen, also as a service.

  • Sign in once

    Sign in through the browser with your OpenAEC account; the keys are kept in your operating system's keychain and renewed automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Which files can I sync and share?

Any kind of file, from drawings and IFC models to spreadsheets and photos. Only temporary files, whatever you put in .openaecignore and names Windows cannot handle are skipped. The assistant reads PDF, Word, Excel, text and images.

Do I need to install anything?

Yes, the OpenAEC Desktop App for Windows, macOS or Linux. Then you sign in to your environment through the browser and link the spaces you want. For servers without a screen there is the openaec-storage command-line program.

What does it cost?

The app is free and open source. You do need an account in the OpenAEC SuperCloud; you sign up on the SuperCloud page, which also says how much storage comes with an account. The assistant uses your environment's AI credit.

Where are my files stored?

In the storage of your own environment in the OpenAEC SuperCloud, in the EU, together with their versions. On your computer they sit in ordinary folders in their own format: there is no separate file format.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows (installer or MSI), macOS (universal .dmg for Intel and Apple Silicon) and Linux (.deb or AppImage). Sync, sharing and sending work everywhere. Status icons, files on demand and the place in the navigation pane are still Windows-only.

Does it fit how construction projects work?

Yes. You work with a space per project, with a fixed folder layout, and give outside parties such as a contractor or reviewer a guest role or a link with an expiry date. Locking shows who is working on a drawing or calculation report.

What can an AI assistant do with it?

The app has its own assistant that reads your files as attachments. An MCP connection that lets an external AI assistant work in your spaces itself does not exist yet; for scripts there is the openaec-storage command-line program.

Does it work with Nextcloud?

No. The first versions were meant for Nextcloud, but the desktop app now works with the OpenAEC SuperCloud's own storage: spaces, roles, versions and transfers.

Can I work without internet?

Yes, with the files that are on your computer. As soon as you are connected again, your changes go to the server. Online-only files open again once you are connected and the app is running.

What does a recipient without an account see?

A download page in your own environment, with only what you shared or sent. If you set a password, they have to enter it first; the password is never in the e-mail. After the expiry date, or once you withdraw the link, it stops working.

Technology

For those who want to know how it is built.

Tauri 2 and Rust

A small native app for Windows, macOS and Linux, with signed installers and automatic updates.

SolidJS

The app's windows: overview, spaces, sharing, sending, assistant and tools.

openaec-sync core

A Rust library without UI: BLAKE3 hashes per block, delta uploads, parallel transfers and conflict rules.

OpenAEC Storage

The server side: a Frappe app for spaces, permissions, versions and transfers; the files themselves sit in S3 storage in the EU.

Windows Cloud Filter API

Status icons, files on demand and the navigation pane, plus its own item in the Windows 11 right-click menu.

OAuth 2 with PKCE

Sign-in through the browser; tokens in the operating system's keychain, with no secrets in the app.
