Open Pointcloud Studio

From laser scan to measurement, drawing and mesh, in one free app

Open a scan of millions of points and look around in it straight away. Measure what you need, cut a plan or section from the cloud and take it to your CAD program as DXF or DWG, or turn it into a mesh or the faces of a room.

Features

Everything for your laser scans, in one app

Start with the highlights, or pick a topic.

01 View

Even a scan of a hundred million points stays smooth

The app builds an index on disk and loads the detail where you look, up to ten million points in view at once. Colour the points by RGB, elevation, intensity or class, and switch classes such as vegetation or ground off one by one.

Learn more

02 Measure

Measure in the scan instead of on site

Click two points and read the length, the horizontal distance and the height difference. Areas are measured in their own plane, so a wall or a sloped roof is right too. Select, delete or thin points; your source file is never touched.

Learn more

03 Section box

Cut out exactly the part you need

A box with six draggable faces limits what you see, measure, export and draw. If a building stands at an angle in the scan, Align to walls turns the box along its walls.

Learn more

04 Drawings

From scan to plan and section in DWG

Make a plan, elevation or section at 1:1, with filled walls and floors and straight, editable CAD lines on a layer of their own. Save it as DXF or DWG, or place it on a sheet from A4 to A0 and export to PDF.

Learn more

05 Mesh and faces

A closed mesh or the faces of a room

Choose from four methods: a closed mesh of a room with its measured deviation, a terrain mesh, a quick 3D surface, or the floors, walls and columns as separate faces with their areas. Export to OBJ, PLY, STL, DXF, DWG or IFC4.

Learn more

06 Photos

Stand where the scanner stood

Click a scan station and look around in its panorama photo, which lies over the points so you can measure there too. Put the photo beside the 3D model, side by side or stacked, or give a scan without colours the colours of its photos.

Learn more

  • All common scan formats

    LAS, LAZ, E57, PLY, PCD, PTX, PTS and text files, plus meshes in OBJ, PLY, OFF and STL. Just drag them onto the window.

  • A whole scan project at once

    Open a folder of scans or a scan project file (.rcp): all scans open together, each as a layer you show or hide.

  • Large scans without waiting

    You see the points while the file is still being read. The index on disk is reused, so a scan opens in a blink the next time.

  • Four colour modes and classes

    RGB, elevation, intensity or class, with eye-dome lighting and an adjustable point size. Switch each class on or off on its own.

  • Stations, photos and walking

    Scan stations from E57, PCD and PTX are shown. Stand in a panorama, step through the photos and walk through the scan with W, A, S and D.

  • Everything in tabs

    The 3D model, views, plans, sections, sheets and photos sit side by side as tabs and come back at the next start.

  • Distance and area

    Length, horizontal length and height difference; area in its own plane and seen from above, with the perimeter. Points come from the whole scan, not only from what is on screen.

  • Select and clean up

    Select with a rectangle, delete with undo, thin to an exact percentage, or move and scale a cloud. An export writes the result.

  • Views, notes and BCF

    Save the camera, section box and colour as a view, add notes and arrows, and send it all as BCF 2.1 to your BIM program.

  • Rotatable section box

    Type the limits as coordinates, fit the box to a selection or let it align to the walls. Where it cuts a mesh, the cut looks solid.

  • Plan, elevation and section

    A 2D drawing at 1:1 as DXF or DWG (R2004 to R2018), with a filled cut, open door and window openings and straight CAD lines.

  • View the drawing right away

    Every drawing opens in the app itself, with layers, scale bar and coordinates. Open CAD Studio comes with it to view DXF and DWG.

  • Sheets from A4 to A0

    Place views and drawings to scale on a sheet with a title block, add dimensions, text and leaders, and export to PDF.

  • Closed mesh of a room

    A surface without overlaps, with door and window openings left open. You see the mean, the 95% and the largest distance between points and mesh.

  • Terrain mesh and 3D surface

    A ground surface from the lowest points, or a quick impression of a whole scan with walls and overhangs.

  • Detect faces and columns

    Floors, ceilings, walls and sloped faces with their outlines and openings, and round columns and pipes, each with its area and residual.

  • Mesh with colour and texture

    Write a coloured mesh as OBJ with a PNG texture. Colours from E57 photos are carried into new meshes.

  • To CAD and BIM

    Meshes and detected faces as OBJ, PLY, STL, as 3D geometry in DXF or DWG, or as IFC4 elements with their measured values.

  • Export and merge

    The whole cloud, the selection, the section box or every Nth point as LAS, LAZ, E57, PLY, XYZ, PTS or CSV, with every point of the source. Merge LAS and LAZ scans.

  • 3D BAG buildings

    Fetch building models of the Netherlands from the 3D BAG for an area up to 2 by 2 km and show them with your scan.

  • MCP server and command API

    An AI assistant or your own script opens scans, measures, draws sections, makes meshes and asks for a picture of the 3D view.

  • Command line without a window

    Convert, crop, merge, index, draw sections, mesh and detect faces in your own scripts.

  • C library and cloud API

    The point-cloud core as a C library for your own software, and as a service over HTTP and MCP in a container.

  • Extensions

    Add tasks of your own with a script in any language, such as Python or PowerShell, that drives the window through the command API.

  • Dutch and English, five themes

    The interface follows the language of your system. Light, Forge, OpenAEC, Blueprint and high contrast, in the style of all OpenAEC apps.

Frequently asked questions

Which files can I open?

Point clouds in LAS, LAZ, E57, PLY, PCD, PTX, PTS and text (XYZ, ASC, TXT, CSV): the open formats that laser scanners and scan software export. Also meshes in OBJ, PLY, OFF and STL, points and faces from an ASCII DXF, and the scan list of a scan project file (.rcp). It does not open the closed scan copies (.rcs) or DWG files.

Do I need to install anything?

Yes, it is a desktop app; there is no web version. The Windows installer works without administrator rights, and the Linux AppImage starts without installing. Open CAD Studio, to view drawings, is already included.

What does Open Pointcloud Studio cost?

Nothing. The app is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 and the point-cloud core under the LGPL-3.0, with no account or subscription.

In which format is my work saved?

In open formats, and your scan itself never changes. You export what you make as LAS, LAZ, E57, PLY, XYZ, PTS or CSV, drawings as DXF, DWG or PDF, meshes and faces as OBJ, PLY, STL, JSON or IFC4, and views as BCF. The app keeps views and notes per scan; there is no project file of its own.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), macOS 11 and later (Apple and Intel) and Linux x86-64 (.deb and AppImage), with a Vulkan driver or OpenGL ES 3. The release page also has a zip without an installer and experimental packages for Linux on 64-bit ARM.

Does it fit building practice?

Yes. Drawings go to your CAD program as DXF or DWG, faces and meshes to BIM as IFC4, and remarks as BCF 2.1. Buildings from the 3D BAG are fetched in Dutch RD coordinates. Measurements are in metres; the coordinates of a scan are not converted.

What can an AI assistant do with it?

open-pointcloud-studio --mcp starts an MCP server (Model Context Protocol). With it, an assistant such as Claude can open scans, set the camera and the section box, measure, export, draw sections, make meshes and detect faces, and get a picture of the 3D view back. Every window also has a local command API for your own scripts.

How large can a scan be?

Large. A 3.2 GiB PLY with 114 million points opens with its index in a fraction of a second, and selecting in a box of 100 by 100 by 100 m takes about half a second. An index can take several gigabytes on disk for a large survey.

How reliable are the filled cut, the closed mesh and the faces?

They are derived from the points and so far measured on generated rooms, not yet on a scan of a real building. A wall scanned from one side becomes a thin strip, and narrow strips such as door reveals do not become a face. That is why the app shows the measured deviation with every mesh; check the result before you use it.

Does it make a complete floor plan or a BIM model automatically?

Not yet. You make plans and sections yourself with the section box, and detected faces go along as separate IFC4 elements. The Pointcloud to Drawing wizard now finds the levels of a building; the steps for walls, openings and the IFC model are still being built.

Technology

For those who want to know how it is built.

Rust

One native program without a browser or web view, for Windows, macOS and Linux, with small, signed packages.

iced and openaec-ui

The interface in iced, with the shared Rust crate openaec-ui for the title strip, ribbon, tabs, panels and themes, as in the other OpenAEC desktop apps.

wgpu

Draws millions of points and meshes on the graphics card through Vulkan, Metal, DirectX or OpenGL ES.

Octree index on disk

Supplies the detail for the current viewpoint within a point budget and is kept for the next time.

Open CAD Studio

The open-source CAD app by Hakan Seven and contributors comes in every package and shows exported DXF and DWG drawings.

MCP, API, CLI and C

An MCP server, a local command API, a command line, a cloud API in a container and a C library (ABI 1.0) under the LGPL.
