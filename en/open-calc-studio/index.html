Open Calc Studio

Build a complete construction estimate, without licence fees or closed formats

Features

From first estimate to tender sheet, in one app

Start with the highlights, or pick a topic.

01 Prompt & Estimate

Type what you want, see what it costs first

Ask in plain language to add a line or look up a total. You first get a proposal with the direct costs, the total and the VAT before and after, and per changed row what changes. Apply it in one go or reject it.

Learn more

02 Guided estimate

Step by step to a sound estimate

Five steps take you through project, quantities, costs, markups and VAT, and a final check. Every change appears straight away in the full estimate, and the total at the bottom keeps up.

Learn more

03 Exchange

Bring your work from your old estimating program

Read estimates from WPCalc, BasCalc, IBIS-TRAD, CUF-XML, STABU and RAW, or from Excel with your own columns. Spanish .bc3, Austrian ÖNORM and German GAEB bills of quantities go in too. Deliver again as WPCalc, CUF, .bc3, ÖNORM or IFC.

Learn more

04 Reports

A clean estimate for your client

Seven reports, from work description and main contract to tender sheet and post-calculation. Choose the report language separately from the app language: headings, units, numbers and dates follow it. Print, or create a PDF in the desktop app.

Learn more

  • Chapters, items and calculation lines

    A clear structure with automatic numbering. Drag rows, also to another chapter.

  • Norms and hourly rates

    A production norm per line and rate groups with their own hourly rates. Change an hours total and the norms scale along.

  • Markups

    General costs, overhead, guarantees, risk, profit, insurance and rounding, in the usual order.

  • Standard and reduced VAT

    Choose the rate per chapter, item or line; the totals use the right rate.

  • Variants and versions

    Put options side by side, keep snapshots and see which rows have changed.

  • Spreadsheets

    Sub-calculations with formulas inside the estimate itself, in a tab beside it.

  • Prompt & Estimate

    Questions and commands in plain language, with a financial proposal you review first. Free-form commands need an OpenAEC account or your own API key.

  • Guided estimate

    Five steps from project details to checks, on the same estimate as the full editor.

  • Review center

    Checks prices, sources and units of the active variant, and jumps to the row with the issue.

  • MCP for AI assistants

    An assistant such as Claude creates estimates, adds items, sets markups and produces a PDF.

  • WPCalc, BasCalc, IBIS-TRAD and CUF

    Read estimates from well-known Dutch estimating programs; write WPCalc and CUF back too.

  • STABU and RAW

    A STABU specification (.s01, .sufx) or RAW specification (.rsx, .rsu) as the basis for your estimate, and a RAW tender sheet as Excel.

  • FIEBDC-3 (.bc3)

    The Spanish exchange format in and out, tested on 22 real files: all amounts equal after the round trip.

  • ÖNORM A 2063

    Read Austrian bills of quantities (.onlv) and standard texts (.onlb), and export .onlv following the official schemas.

  • GAEB and XPWE

    Read German GAEB bills of quantities (.x83) and Italian and Czech estimates (XPWE, XC4).

  • Excel, CSV and price lists

    Map the columns of an Excel or CSV file yourself, and load price lists (ZSX, BMEcat) into your library.

  • IFC cost schedule

    Read and write an estimate as IfcCostSchedule, for exchange with BIM software.

  • Seven reports

    Work description, main contract, subcontracting, tender sheet, post-calculation and two extended overviews.

  • Report in your client's language

    Headings, units and the notation of numbers and dates follow the chosen report language.

  • Print and PDF

    With cover page, summary, page numbering and portrait or landscape. The PDF preview is in the desktop app.

  • Quantities from a 3D model

    Open an IFC file, click elements and take their quantities into the selected item.

  • Measure on a PDF drawing

    Set the scale with two points, measure lengths and areas, and keep the measurements with the estimate.

  • Origin of every quantity

    See whether a quantity comes from a PDF, IFC model or spreadsheet, with page or object and a warning when the source is missing.

  • Workspaces and second screen

    Save your layout for measuring, estimating or checking, and put the drawing on a second screen.

  • 39 languages and five themes

    Including right-to-left for Arabic, Persian, Hebrew and Urdu, and light, dark, Blueprint and high contrast.

Frequently asked questions

Which files can I open?

Your own .ifcCalc files, estimates from WPCalc, BasCalc, IBIS-TRAD and CUF-XML, STABU specifications (.s01, .sufx), RAW specifications (.rsx, .rsu), Spanish .bc3, Austrian ÖNORM (.onlv, .onlb), German GAEB (.x83), Italian and Czech XPWE and XC4, IFC with a cost schedule, and Excel or CSV. Price lists come in as ZSX or BMEcat.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The web version works straight in the browser and your files stay on your own computer. For the PDF preview of reports there is the desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux.

What does Open Calc Studio cost?

Nothing. The app is free and open source under the LGPL-3.0 licence, with no subscription. Only free-form commands to the AI assistant use credit from your OpenAEC account or your own API key.

In which format is my work saved?

In an .ifcCalc file: open, readable JSON with a documented structure. Older files always open; the desktop app saves quietly every two minutes.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows (.exe or .msi), macOS on Apple Silicon (.dmg) and Linux (AppImage, .deb, .rpm and Snap), and any modern browser. The Mac version is not yet notarized by Apple: the first time, you allow it once under Privacy & Security.

Does it fit construction practice?

Yes. The structure with norms, rate groups, markups and standard and reduced VAT follows Dutch practice, and STABU, RAW and the tender sheet go in or out. For Spain, Austria and Germany there are .bc3, ÖNORM and GAEB.

What can an AI assistant do with it?

In the app, Prompt & Estimate helps with questions and proposals you review first. There is also an MCP server (Model Context Protocol): with it, an assistant such as Claude creates estimates, adds items, sets markups and produces a PDF.

Are the newest features already in the desktop app?

Not yet. Prompt & Estimate, the guided estimate, the review center, measuring on PDF and quantities from a 3D model are in the web version now. They come to the installers with the next release; v0.13.0 does not have them yet.

Which formats can I export to?

PDF, CSV, IFC, WPCalc, CUF-XML, .bc3, ÖNORM (.onlv) and the RAW tender sheet as Excel. Export to IBIS-TRAD and RAW-RSX is limited; there is no GAEB export yet.

Is it ready for real work?

It is in public testing: stable enough to work with, but check your results and expect changes. Report what you find on GitHub; testers with .bc3 files or a Mac are especially welcome.

Technology

For those who want to know how it is built.

Tauri 2 en Rust

A native desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux, with calculations and PDF layout in Rust.

React en TypeScript

The same interface in the browser and on the desktop, with a virtualized grid for large estimates.

Typst

Modern typesetting for the PDF reports, with clean tables and page numbering.

Open .ifcCalc format

Readable JSON with its own versioning policy and automatic migration of older files.

MCP server

Creates and edits estimate files from a conversation with an AI assistant, all the way to the PDF.

Embedding

A React/JavaScript SDK, iframe embedding and a native library (DLL, .so, .dylib) in the source code, for your own software.
