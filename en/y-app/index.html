Y-app

Your whole working day on one screen: projects, hours, mail and calendar

Y-app is a clear workspace on top of ERPNext. You install it on your own ERPNext site and sign in with your existing account; your data stays where it is.

01 Mail

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

Yes, on the server: Y-app is an app for an existing ERPNext 16 site. Your administrator installs it with bench get-app and install-app (see the README). You then open it in the browser under /y-next and sign in with your ERPNext account. There is no public demo yet.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Y-app is open source under the MIT licence. You only pay for hosting ERPNext, if you don't do that yourself.

Where is my work stored?

Simply in your ERPNext database. Y-app reads and writes through ERPNext's standard API; there is no separate database, no separate account and no external server.

Which systems does it run on?

In any modern browser, including tablets and phones. The server needs Frappe 16 and ERPNext 16; nothing more.

Do I need ERPNext?

Yes. Y-app is a different, simpler front end for ERPNext. All your projects, hours and invoices live in ERPNext; Y-app makes them easier to use.

Does every employee see everything?

No. Y-app follows ERPNext roles and permissions. An employee sees their own tasks, hours and leave; an employer also sees finance, approvals and staff.

Can I edit Word and Excel files with it?

Not yet in version 0.40.0. The development version already has Calc and Writer for .xlsx and .docx, plus Open CAD Studio, Open Planner Studio and an IFC viewer; they will come in a later release.

Is there a hosted version?

Y-app started as a hosted service in which you link several ERPNext environments. That version is not distributed publicly. You install the open version on this page on your own ERPNext site; get in touch if you want help with the installation.

Technology

For those who want to know how it is built.

Frappe-app

An installable app for Frappe 16 and ERPNext 16, with its own doctypes, fields and permissions.

React 19 + TypeScript

The interface is built with React, TypeScript and Vite and ships pre-built; no Node needed on the server.

Tailwind CSS 4

Styling with Tailwind; the interface adapts to desktop, tablet and phone.

Frappe REST API

Reads and writes with the signed-in user's session; ERPNext permissions stay in charge.

JMAP en CalDAV

Optional link to a mail server such as Stalwart for calendars, invitations and out-of-office.

MIT

Open source under the MIT licence. Development happens in Y-app-ERPNext, releases are in y_next.
