Open Infra Studio

GIS and civil design in one drawing, wherever in the world your project is

A free plugin for Open CAD Studio. Pick the project location, bring in maps, open data and 3D city models, and design alignments, profiles and corridors in the same DWG. In the Netherlands, BGT, cadastre and KLIC on NLCS layers come on top.

Features

From existing situation to design, in your drawing

Start with the highlights, or pick a topic.

01 Maps worldwide

Over 600 map services in 45 countries, no API key needed

Search by place or theme, in other languages too, and see straight away which services cover your project location. Aerial photos, cadastral maps and vector data arrive in the drawing reprojected to your project's coordinate system, like the Paris cadastre over the aerial photo here.

Learn more

02 Project location

Put your project on the map, in any coordinate system

Click on the map or search an address, place, parcel or coordinate. Pick a built-in system such as RD New, Lambert-93 or British National Grid, let the app choose the right UTM zone, or fetch any other EPSG code. The location is saved in the drawing.

Learn more

03 BGT, cadastre and KLIC

A site drawing on NLCS layers in five commands

BGT, cadastral boundaries and a KLIC delivery each land on their own NLCS layer, with colour, linetype and the source's object data. A drawing sheet with title block, legend, north arrow and scale bar finishes it, and your design goes back as a BGT mutation.

Learn more

04 Civil design

From survey points to a corridor with earthworks

Import survey points or LandXML, build a terrain model with contours and draw an alignment with stationing. Length, design and cross sections follow, and a corridor with side slopes down to the ground gives the earthworks per cross section and a mass balance.

Learn more

05 The subsurface

Cables, pipes and CPTs in 3D under a see-through ground

A KLIC delivery becomes tubes at their depth, or at the usual cover where the delivery does not give it. CPTs and boreholes from the BRO become columns of soil layers. Make the ground see-through and look at it all from below.

Learn more

06 Historical maps

Two centuries of maps as an underlay

Every Dutch topographic map from 1815 to 2014 and the cadastral maps of 1832, behind your drawing in one click. Natura 2000, the geomorphological map and the soil map are ready too.

Learn more

  • Search map services

    600 checked WMS, WFS and tile services in 45 countries. Search by place or theme and see what covers your location.

  • Aerial photos and maps

    Any WMS or tile server: browse the layers, preview them and put the image behind the drawing as an underlay.

  • Vector data with attributes

    WFS and OGC API Features: pick a feature type, test it on the map and keep the attributes as object data.

  • OpenStreetMap, worldwide

    Buildings, roads, rail, water, structures, land use, cables and pipelines, fences and house numbers, anywhere in the world.

  • Any coordinate system

    14 built-in systems, the right UTM zone automatically anywhere, and any other EPSG code, saved with the drawing.

  • Open GIS files

    GeoJSON, Shapefile, GeoPackage, KML, GPX, GML, GeoTIFF, ASCII grid and LAS/LAZ, reprojected to your project location.

  • Historical maps

    Topographic maps from 1815 to 2014 and the cadastral maps of 1832 as an underlay.

  • BGT and cadastre on NLCS

    42 BGT collections and the cadastral map, each object on the right NLCS layer per the official NLCS-BGT mapping.

  • KLIC deliveries

    Open the delivery as zip or xml. Cables and pipes land per theme on their layer, with network operator, diameter and material.

  • Drawing sheet in one click

    Frame, title block with the sources, legend of the layers used, north arrow, scale bar and RD crosses. A3 to A0 is chosen for you.

  • NLCS check and quantities

    Check and repair the drawing against NLCS 5.02, and count quantities per layer or object as CSV or as a table in the drawing.

  • Design back to the BGT

    Your design on NLCS layers as a BGT mutation in IMGeo-GML 2.1.1, with a report of anything ambiguous.

  • Heights and point cloud from AHN

    Spot heights, contour lines and length profiles from AHN4, and the point cloud of just your project area in 3D.

  • Survey points and LandXML

    PNEZD and PENZD files with automatic detection of separator and order, and LandXML with points, surfaces and alignments.

  • Terrain model

    A TIN from points, 3D lines, contours or AHN, with contour lines and a height analysis in colour bands.

  • Alignments and profiles

    Alignments with stationing and curve radii, length profile, design profile with vertical curves and cross sections in a grid.

  • Corridors and earthworks

    Templates for road, cycle path, motorway and dike, side slopes to the ground, cut and fill per cross section and a mass balance.

  • A 3D city in one command

    Terrain with the aerial photo, every building from 3D BAG, the whole BGT in 3D, trees and water at its level.

  • CityGML and CityJSON

    3D city models from anywhere, read only within your project area: a 97 MB city tile loads in under two seconds.

  • Subsurface in 3D

    KLIC cables and pipes at depth and BRO CPTs and boreholes as soil columns, under a see-through ground.

  • Water level

    Lay a water surface at a chosen level over the project area and see which part lies lower.

  • IFC 4.3 export

    The whole drawing as IFC 4.3: every object its own IFC class, alignments as IfcAlignment, georeferenced and with object data.

  • An AI assistant builds along

    An MCP server with 39 tools lets an AI assistant build whole models, from project location to corridor.

  • Scriptable commands

    Every command takes arguments and writes its result as JSON, with handles, lengths and volumes.

  • English and Dutch

    The interface follows Open CAD Studio's language. Switch the Dutch modules off if you do not need them.

Frequently asked questions

Which files can I open?

Open CAD Studio opens the drawing itself (DWG and DXF). Open Infra Studio also reads GeoJSON, Shapefile, GeoPackage, KML/KMZ, GPX, GML and CSV with coordinates; GeoTIFF, ASCII grid and JPEG/PNG with a world file; LAS and LAZ; survey points (PNEZD, PENZD); LandXML 1.x; CityGML, CityJSON and IMGeo-GML; and KLIC deliveries (IMKL 2.0). Not yet: reading JPEG 2000, E57, PLY and IFC.

Do I need to install anything?

Yes. First install Open CAD Studio v2026.43. Type PLUGINS there, pick Open Infra Studio and click Install: that is the GIS plugin. Then download the Civiel plugin above, unzip it into <config>/OpenCADStudio/plugins/ and restart Open CAD Studio. The ribbon then shows the GIS and Civiel tabs.

What does Open Infra Studio cost?

Nothing. Open Infra Studio is free and open source under the GPL-3.0, like Open CAD Studio. Most map services are open data; credit the source, the title block does that for you. Only the Sentinel-2 satellite background is limited to non-commercial use.

In which file format is my work saved?

Everything lives in Open CAD Studio's DWG, including the project location and the object data. Map images are saved as PNG beside the drawing. You can export to IFC 4.3, a BGT mutation (IMGeo-GML), CSV for survey points, mass balance and quantities, and PDF via Open CAD Studio. Export to LandXML, GeoJSON, Shapefile or GeoPackage is not available yet.

Which systems does it run on?

Wherever Open CAD Studio runs: Windows and Linux (x86-64) and macOS on Apple silicon. On Windows and Linux everything works. On macOS the ribbon buttons and commands work, but the workbench window does not yet.

Does it fit civil engineering practice?

Yes. NLCS 5.02 is built in, with layers, colours and linetypes per drawing status. BGT, BRK, KLIC (IMKL 2.0), AHN and BRO come straight from the source in RD and NAP, and a design goes back to the BGT as IMGeo-GML 2.1.1. Outside the Netherlands you work in the national grid or in UTM. Note: reprojecting to RD yourself is accurate to about 0.2 metres; fine for underlays, but use data already in RD for setting out.

What can an AI assistant do with it?

A lot. An MCP server with 39 tools lets an assistant such as Claude build whole models: project location, maps, BGT, 3D buildings, alignments, corridors and IFC export. Every command takes arguments and returns its result as JSON, so your own scripts can use it too.

Does it work outside the Netherlands?

Yes. Project location, coordinate systems, the service browser, OpenStreetMap, GIS files, CityGML and the whole civil design work anywhere. Only the Dutch base data and NLCS sit in a group of their own, which you can switch off.

Do I need an internet connection?

For map services, open data, 3D BAG, AHN and address search, yes. Files you already have, such as GIS files, survey points, LandXML, CityGML and a KLIC delivery, and the civil design work offline. Data loads in the background, so the drawing stays responsive.

Technology

For those who want to know how it is built.

Rust

About 29,000 lines of Rust and 120 unit tests, in one Cargo workspace with two plugins: GIS and Civiel.

Open CAD Studio plugin API

The plugin runs in its own process beside the drawing and talks to Open CAD Studio v2026.43 through plugin API 13.

egui

The workbench window with map, search and settings, with network work on its own threads so the CAD window stays responsive.

proj4rs · epsg.io

Coordinate transformations in pure Rust; systems that are not built in are fetched once from epsg.io.

Open standards

WMS, WFS, OGC API Features, WCS, COPC, CityGML, CityJSON, LandXML, IMKL 2.0, IMGeo-GML 2.1.1 · IFC 4.3 (IFC4X3_ADD2)

MCP

A Python MCP server with 39 tools that finds every Open CAD Studio window by itself.
